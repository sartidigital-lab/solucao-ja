### Priority Issues
*   **[P1] Gradient Text**: Clutters headings across 10 routes.
    *   *Why it matters*: Violates the "Clean Curator" root system design principles.
    *   *Fix*: Modify header typography to use solid ink (`text-ink`) weights.
    *   *Suggested command*: `$impeccable quieter apps/web`
*   **[P1] Typographic Scale Drift**: Small literal inputs (`text-[9px]`, `text-[10px]`) cause readability failure on mobile.
    *   *Why it matters*: Users with standard sight or low Android display settings cannot distinguish critical metadata.
    *   *Fix*: Set the minimum step to `text-xs` (12px) and use standardized tailwind components.
    *   *Suggested command*: `$impeccable typeset apps/web`
*   **[P1] Poor Color Contrast**: Reading grey/dark slate text on teal and amber profile sections.
    *   *Why it matters*: Fails WCAG AA requirements for contrast ratios.
    *   *Fix*: Replace slate overrides with deep tints (`text-teal-980` or `text-white` / `text-[oklch(0.20_0.05_145)]`).
    *   *Suggested command*: `$impeccable colorize apps/web`
*   **[P1] Flat-By-Default Card Violations**: Combining fine borders with soft shadows.
    *   *Why it matters*: Creates visual noise and inconsistent depth patterns.
    *   *Fix*: Remove the duplicate shadow overrides to stick with clean borders or solid cards.
    *   *Suggested command*: `$impeccable layout apps/web`
*   **[P2] Mobile Target Failures**: Booking actions and secondary links use less than 44x44px.
    *   *Why it matters*: Mobile users on-the-go experience high friction tapping filters and detail views.
    *   *Fix*: Add padding to secondary actions like `Ver serviços` and toggle indicators.
    *   *Suggested command*: `$impeccable adapt apps/web`
